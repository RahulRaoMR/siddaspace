Meet Siddaspace

An interior design and execution company in Bangalore

Siddaspace brings residential interiors, commercial fit-outs, custom furniture, construction and renovation together through design and coordinated site execution.

Less talk. More execution.

Design decisions and site execution belong in the same plan.

From our Ganapathipura office on Kanakapura Main Road, we discuss your drawings, room requirements, materials and budget before defining the work.

Backed by a dedicated manufacturing facility and an experienced execution team, we coordinate work from individual modular kitchens to complete home interiors and corporate office fit-outs.

Meet the Siddaspace team

From your first plan to the finished space

  1. Consultation: discuss the property, rooms, intended use, budget and timeline.
  2. Measurement and design: review dimensions, layouts, storage, materials and finishes.
  3. Quotation: agree quantities, inclusions, exclusions and responsibilities.
  4. Production and site work: coordinate approved furniture, civil work and site readiness.
  5. Installation and handover: review the finished work and applicable project agreement.
