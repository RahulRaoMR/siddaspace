Make your first discussion useful

Bring a floor plan or approximate dimensions, photographs of the current space, your possession or move-in timeline, and a budget range. If you have reference images, explain which details you like: storage, finishes, lighting or the overall mood.

For an occupied home, tell us what needs to remain usable during the work. For an apartment, share any building access or working-hour requirements. These details help us discuss a realistic scope before arranging site measurements.
