Interior design cost guide

How to plan 2 BHK and 3 BHK interior design costs in Bangalore

A useful interior budget starts with a measured scope. Compare the rooms, furniture, materials and site work behind a quotation before deciding which package suits your home.

A planning guide from Siddaspace. Discuss your project requirements.

What affects home interior cost?

Home interior cost is the combined cost of the agreed furniture quantities, materials, finishes, site work and installation. Two Bangalore homes with the same BHK label can need different scopes. Begin with a room-by-room list

Write down what each room needs before comparing prices. A 2 BHK apartment may need a kitchen, two wardrobes, a TV unit and a study. A 3 BHK may add another wardrobe, a flexible guest room or more storage. These are planning examples; your own furniture list should follow the way you use the home.

Separate essential items from optional upgrades. This makes it easier to adjust a budget without losing the storage and layouts you need every day.

Build the estimate from quantities and specifications

A working budget can be organized as furniture and cabinetry quantities × agreed rates, plus civil work, electrical and plumbing changes, lighting, installation, transport and applicable taxes. Confirm how each supplier measures and prices its work before comparing totals.

For each wardrobe or kitchen unit, compare dimensions, internal fittings, board specifications, shutter finish and hardware. A lower total can reflect fewer units or a different specification rather than a like-for-like saving.

Why two homes of the same BHK size can cost differently

  • Scope: a kitchen-and-wardrobe package differs from a complete home with ceilings, lighting and custom furniture.
  • Materials: board, finish and hardware selections affect both appearance and cost.
  • Site condition: a new apartment may need less preparation than an older flat with repairs and service changes.
  • Detailing: curved furniture, special finishes and more complex installations need separate assessment.
  • Access and sequence: building rules, occupied rooms and phased work can affect execution planning.

Questions to ask before approving a quotation

  1. Are the room list, unit dimensions and material specifications documented?
  2. Are countertop, sink, appliances and loose furniture included or excluded?
  3. Who handles dismantling, debris removal, painting and final touch-ups?
  4. Are installation, transport and taxes shown clearly?
  5. How will design changes and additional site work be approved?
  6. What milestones, warranty terms and handover checks apply?

Plan an affordable scope without guessing the final price

Keep essential storage and useful layouts at the centre of your brief. Compare simpler finishes, standard hardware options and phased decorative additions with the team. For a premium or luxury home, identify the details that matter most so custom work is planned deliberately.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 2 BHK interior cost in Bangalore?

A final amount requires the kitchen and furniture dimensions, material choices, finish level and any site work. Prepare the same room list for each quote so the comparison is meaningful.

Is renovation included in a complete home interior price?

Only when the written scope includes it. Dismantling, repairs, waterproofing, flooring, electrical and plumbing changes should be identified separately.

Does the estimate planner provide a binding price?

No. It gathers your requirements for discussion with Siddaspace. Final pricing follows site measurements, approved specifications and the written quotation.
