Home interior styles

Choose a modern, minimalist or Scandinavian interior for your home

A style reference is a starting point for decisions about layout, storage, materials and light. Use these ideas to explain the mood you want, then adapt the details to your Bangalore home and everyday routine.

A planning guide from Siddaspace. Discuss your project requirements.

Modern and contemporary interiors

A modern interior brief often begins with clear lines, useful furniture and a restrained material palette. Contemporary interiors can be more flexible, mixing current finishes and forms with the pieces you already own.

For either approach, choose the larger elements first: flooring, kitchen fronts, wardrobes and seating. Then review wall treatments, handles, lighting and decor as part of the same room rather than selecting every finish separately.

Minimalist interiors and space-saving design

A minimalist room works best when storage has been planned carefully. Decide which objects need open display and which should have a closed cupboard or drawer. Fewer furniture pieces can make movement easier when each one serves a clear purpose.

In a compact apartment, consider a combined study and storage wall, a TV unit with concealed cable access, or a dining-side crockery unit. Keep enough clear space for everyday use before adding furniture to every wall.

Scandinavian-inspired home interiors

If your references favour light colours, warm wood tones, simple furniture and soft textures, use that combination to brief the team. Review samples in the room's available daylight and choose finishes that are practical to maintain.

You can introduce the look through selected surfaces and furniture without matching every piece. Plan curtains, lighting and upholstery alongside cabinetry so the home feels cohesive.

Luxury interiors with deliberate details

A luxury brief can focus on custom furniture, refined materials, careful junctions and a coordinated lighting plan. Choose the details that matter to you and understand their maintenance needs before committing to a finish.

For a villa or a larger apartment, consider how the entry, living areas and bedrooms relate to one another. A shared material palette can connect rooms while allowing different uses and moods.

Turn references into a practical design brief

  1. Choose a small set of reference images and mark the details you like.
  2. List the existing furniture you want to keep.
  3. Describe your storage, work-from-home and family needs.
  4. Review a measured furniture layout before final finishes.
  5. Compare material samples and the written scope against your budget.
