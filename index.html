Helpful answers

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I choose the best interior designer for my Bangalore home?

Review relevant project work, compare the same room list and material specifications, and ask how site execution, changes and handover are managed. Explore our interior projects and discuss your floor plan with the Siddaspace team.

What does 2 BHK or 3 BHK interior design cost in Bangalore?

Cost depends on furniture quantities, finishes, hardware, lighting and any civil changes. Start with our interior cost guide and quotation planner, then request a measured quotation for your agreed scope.

Do you handle both interior design and renovation?

Yes. Siddaspace coordinates civil work and home renovation with furniture and finishes. Our turnkey interior solutions bring design and execution into one agreed project scope.

Where is your Bangalore interior design office?

Our office is in Ganapathipura on Kanakapura Main Road, Bengaluru 560078. See office details and directions, and call +91 6364309878 to arrange a consultation.

How long does an interior project take?

The schedule depends on the rooms, civil work, design approvals, materials and site readiness. Siddaspace agrees the sequence and delivery milestones for your project before execution.

What is included in the package?

Packages can include modular kitchen, wardrobes, storage units, living room furniture, bedroom furniture, finishes, hardware, and installation depending on the selected scope.

Do you provide warranty?

Warranty eligibility, coverage and exclusions depend on the selected materials and the written project agreement. Ask the team to confirm the applicable terms before approval.

Can I customize the design?

Yes. Every project is customized around your floor plan, storage needs, finish preferences, budget, and lifestyle.

Do you offer site visits?

Yes. We offer site visits for measurements, requirement understanding, and final scope validation before execution.
