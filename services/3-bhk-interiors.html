Give each bedroom a clear purpose

A 3 BHK home gives you more flexibility, but each room still needs a specific brief. One bedroom may need generous wardrobe storage, another may serve children, and a third may double as a guest room and home office.

Plan these uses before deciding on furniture quantities. A study table, a dressing unit and a full-height wardrobe compete for wall space, so review them on a measured layout with bed clearances and window access.

Connect the kitchen, dining and living spaces

Choose a shared palette for cabinetry, furniture and wall finishes, then adapt details to the role of each room. The kitchen and dining area may benefit from coordinated crockery storage; the living room may need a TV unit, display space and comfortable seating for family and guests.

Review lighting and electrical points alongside the furniture plan. False ceiling design should support the lighting layout and maintenance access rather than being treated as a separate decoration decision.

What shapes 3 BHK interior design cost in Bangalore?

The number of wardrobes, kitchen size, custom furniture, shutter finishes and hardware specifications can change the budget considerably. A premium or luxury interior brief may involve more custom detailing and finish coordination; an affordable scope can focus on essential storage and phase optional elements.

Compare proposals using the same room list, unit dimensions and material specifications. Separate renovation work from furniture and interiors, and check installation, transport, taxes and exclusions. A price attached only to the label “3 BHK” does not describe the complete scope.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a 3 BHK interior cost in Bangalore?

The cost follows the actual furniture list, materials, lighting and site work. Compare the same dimensions and specifications rather than prices attached only to the BHK label.

Does a complete package include every room and appliance?

The written scope determines inclusions. Confirm room furniture, ceilings, lights, appliances, decor and civil work separately before approval.

Can each bedroom have a different purpose?

Yes. Define the main bedroom, children’s room, guest room or study needs first, then coordinate storage, finishes and furniture across the home.

Can Siddaspace coordinate the full execution?

Yes. Discuss turnkey coordination for design, furniture, site work, installation and handover, with responsibilities and exclusions agreed in advance.
