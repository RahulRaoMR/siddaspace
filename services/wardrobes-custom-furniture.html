Wardrobes planned from the inside out

Start with what you need to store: hanging clothes, folded clothes, luggage, accessories or shared family storage. Internal shelves, drawer sizes and hanging heights matter as much as the shutter finish.

Hinged wardrobes need space for doors to open; sliding shutters need careful access planning. A walk-in wardrobe works best when the room can accommodate both storage and comfortable movement. Bring room measurements so we can review the options with your bed and other furniture in place.

Furniture for living, dining and study spaces

  • TV unit design: coordinate screen position, cable access, display shelves and concealed storage.
  • Crockery units: plan everyday access, serving surfaces and display storage around the dining area.
  • Pooja units: discuss placement, storage, lighting and the needs of your household before finalizing the design.
  • Study tables: allow for legroom, equipment, task lighting, power points and books.
  • Bedroom furniture: coordinate bedsides, dressing units and wardrobes with the room's circulation.

Space-saving furniture begins with a clear purpose for each unit. A combined study and storage wall can be more useful than several separate pieces in a compact apartment.

Materials, finishes and installation details

Compare board specifications, shutter finishes, handles, hinges and drawer hardware in the written quotation. Discuss how the furniture will meet skirting, uneven walls, electrical points and other site conditions.

Custom furniture cost depends on unit size, internal fittings, finish complexity and installation requirements. Review the full room before approving individual units so the furniture feels coordinated and stays within your budget.

Bring your room plan to the consultation

Share photographs, approximate dimensions, storage priorities and reference styles. Our team can discuss the furniture scope and arrange measurements before final drawings and pricing. For a full apartment, plan the modular kitchen and wardrobes together to compare materials and coordinate installation.

Frequently asked questions

Does Siddaspace make custom wardrobes?

Yes. Wardrobe layouts, internal storage, shutters and hardware are planned around your room and storage requirements.

Can you plan TV units, pooja units and study tables?

Yes. These units can be discussed within a complete home plan or a focused furniture scope, with placement and dimensions confirmed before production.

Is sliding or hinged storage better for my room?

The choice depends on opening space, access and room layout. Review the wardrobe with the bed, doors and other furniture shown on a measured plan.

What should a furniture quotation specify?

It should identify unit dimensions, materials, shutter finishes, internal fittings, hardware and installation responsibilities, with exclusions stated clearly.
