What is included in restaurant interior design?

Restaurant interior design coordinates dining layouts, seating, counters, lighting, ceilings and finishes with guest movement and everyday service. Siddaspace designs and executes customer-facing hospitality spaces in Bangalore. The brief starts with how guests arrive and dine, then connects the furniture, materials and site work to the restaurant’s operating needs and agreed project scope.

Who it is for
Restaurant operators planning a new dining space or updating an existing hospitality interior.
What is included
Dining layout, seating, counters, feature walls, lighting, ceilings and finishes as specified.
Where
Bangalore / Bengaluru. Contact our Ganapathipura office to confirm the site visit and execution arrangements for your property.
What to prepare
Floor plan, seating needs, service flow, brand references and the work to be retained.
Cost factors
Seating and counter quantities, finish specifications, lighting and existing site conditions.
Schedule factors
Layout decisions, services coordination, approvals, material lead times and installation access.

Balance the guest experience with everyday operations

Start with seating requirements, service movement and the way guests arrive, wait and dine. Counters, dining tables and circulation need to work together before decorative finishes are finalized.

Bring the menu format, brand references and an outline of operational needs to your consultation. Coordinate kitchen and service requirements with the relevant project professionals so the customer-facing interior supports the overall premises.

Materials, lighting and hospitality execution

Review upholstery, wall finishes, flooring and lighting against cleaning and maintenance needs. Feature walls and decorative panels should support the brand while keeping movement and service access practical.

Confirm the furniture quantities, finish specifications and installation responsibilities in the quotation. For a renovation, assess existing site conditions and agree how dismantling, services and finishing will be sequenced.

Frequently asked questions

Does Siddaspace handle restaurant execution as well as design?

Yes. Siddaspace’s restaurant service covers planning and interior execution, with deliverables and responsibilities defined in the agreed scope.

Can you plan a restaurant renovation?

Yes. Share the current layout and operational requirements. Existing finishes, services, access and the opening plan affect the renovation sequence.

What affects a restaurant interior quote?

Furniture quantities, counter design, materials, lighting and existing-condition work affect the quote. Kitchen equipment and specialist services should be identified separately where relevant.

What should I prepare for the first discussion?

Bring a floor plan, approximate seating needs, service requirements and reference images. Share the budget, timeline and any landlord or building constraints.
