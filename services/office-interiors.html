What is an office interior fit-out?

An office fit-out turns a workspace brief into a usable arrangement of workstations, cabins, meeting rooms, reception, storage and supporting finishes. Siddaspace plans and executes Bangalore office interiors around team size, visitor movement and operational needs. Furniture, partitions, lighting and services are coordinated within the agreed scope, including phased renovation where the office remains occupied.

Who it is for
Businesses planning a new workspace, additional desks or an occupied-office renovation.
What is included
Workstations, cabins, meeting rooms, reception, storage and pantry or cafeteria details as agreed.
Where
Bangalore / Bengaluru. Contact our Ganapathipura office to confirm the site visit and execution arrangements for your property.
What to prepare
Floor plan, desk count, visitor needs, equipment, building rules and target move-in date.
Cost factors
Furniture quantities, partitions, finishes, service changes and installation phases.
Schedule factors
Design sign-off, equipment details, building access, site readiness and production.

Commercial interiors built around your team

Begin with the number of people using the office, meeting patterns and visitor movement. Workstation layouts need to account for comfortable circulation, storage, power and data access. Cabins and conference rooms should be planned with privacy, equipment and practical furniture clearances in mind.

Include cafeteria and pantry areas, print stations and reception storage in the initial brief. A coordinated layout helps avoid adding furniture and services after the main fit-out decisions have been made.

Office furniture and fit-out coordination

Explore workstations, office chairs, conference tables, reception tables and office storage solutions as part of the same plan. Confirm dimensions, materials and service requirements before procurement.

Discuss partitions, ceiling work, flooring, lighting, painting and service coordination in the written scope. Our turnkey interior service can bring the design and execution brief together.

Office renovation and phased installation

For an occupied office, share operating hours, building access rules and the areas that must remain usable. Agree whether installation will be phased and how existing furniture or equipment will be protected.

Cost depends on floor area, furniture quantities, material specifications and services work. Use the office fit-out planning checklist to prepare your brief, then contact the team with the floor plan and timeline.

Frequently asked questions

Can Siddaspace renovate an occupied office?

Office renovation can be discussed in phases. Share operational hours and areas that must remain usable so the team can plan access, protection and installation.

Do you plan cafeterias and pantry areas?

Yes. Cafeterias and pantry areas are part of Siddaspace’s published commercial capabilities. Confirm furniture, cabinetry and service connections in the proposal.

What should an office fit-out quotation include?

It should define layout, furniture quantities, finishes, partitions, services, installation and exclusions. Use the office fit-out checklist to prepare a comparable brief.

How are office timelines agreed?

The schedule follows the approved layout, procurement needs, service work and access arrangements. The team agrees milestones for your premises rather than one fixed duration.
