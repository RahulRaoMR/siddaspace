What turnkey interior execution can include

A turnkey brief defines who coordinates each part of the project. Depending on the agreed scope, this can include space planning, drawings, material selection, custom furniture production, civil changes, electrical and plumbing coordination, ceilings, painting and installation.

The word “turnkey” does not mean every possible item is included automatically. Confirm the deliverables, exclusions and responsibilities in writing, including any appliances, loose furniture or decor supplied separately.

A clear process from brief to handover

  1. Requirements: share the floor plan, intended use, budget and timeline.
  2. Site assessment: check dimensions, existing services, access and readiness.
  3. Design and specification: review layouts, drawings, materials, finishes and hardware.
  4. Scope approval: agree the quotation, responsibilities, payment stages and change process.
  5. Execution: sequence civil work, services, production and installation.
  6. Handover: review finished work, note outstanding items and confirm applicable warranty details.

Turnkey home and commercial interiors

For a new apartment or villa, coordinated execution helps connect the kitchen, wardrobes, living spaces and room finishes. Plan practical storage and furniture first, then bring lighting and decorative elements into the same design.

For an office, restaurant or other commercial space, begin with the activities the premises must support. Workstation counts, visitor flow, service areas and installation access all need to be considered before procurement. Discuss any project-specific approval requirements with the relevant professionals.

Compare turnkey quotations on the same scope

Review quantities, material brands or specifications, finish options, installation and exclusions. Ask how variations are recorded and approved, and which milestones depend on your decisions or site access.

Delivery dates depend on design sign-off, material availability and site readiness. Confirm a schedule for your own project rather than assuming the same timeline applies to a kitchen upgrade and a complete commercial fit-out.

Frequently asked questions

Does turnkey mean everything is automatically included?

No. The agreed quotation defines inclusions, exclusions and supply responsibilities. Confirm appliances, loose furniture, decor and specialist services explicitly.

What is the difference between design-only and turnkey work?

A design-only brief centres on plans and specifications. A turnkey brief adds responsibility for the agreed production, site coordination, installation and handover.

Can you execute an existing interior design?

Siddaspace’s published execution service works from drawings. Share the plans, specifications and responsibilities so the team can assess the installation and site scope.

How is a turnkey project scheduled?

A schedule is agreed after the drawings, materials and site requirements are understood. Approval dates, procurement and site readiness affect the milestones.
