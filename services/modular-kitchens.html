What is a modular kitchen?

A modular kitchen uses planned cabinet units and fittings arranged around cooking, preparation, cleaning and storage. Siddaspace designs modular kitchens in Bangalore with layouts, cabinetry, appliances and finishes coordinated before production. The kitchen can be part of a complete home interior or a focused upgrade, with plumbing, electrical and countertop work included only where agreed.

Who it is for
Homeowners planning a new kitchen, a complete apartment or an existing-kitchen upgrade.
What is included
Measured cabinet layout, storage, shutters, hardware, appliance clearances and installation as agreed.
Where
Bangalore / Bengaluru. Contact our Ganapathipura office to confirm the site visit and execution arrangements for your property.
What to prepare
Kitchen plan, photographs, appliance dimensions, existing service points and storage needs.

Start with the layout, then choose the finish

Bring a floor plan or photographs of your existing kitchen. We begin with doors, windows, water points and appliance positions before planning cabinet sizes. An L-shaped layout can leave room for dining; a parallel kitchen can keep preparation and cooking zones close; a U-shaped kitchen needs enough clear space for doors and drawers to open comfortably.

For a kitchen renovation, the existing plumbing, electrical points and wall condition affect the scope. Decide early whether those services will stay in place or change with the new layout.

Cabinets, storage and countertop coordination

  • Base and wall cabinets: plan drawer access, overhead reach and everyday storage before adding accessories.
  • Appliances: confirm hob, chimney, refrigerator and dishwasher dimensions before approving the cabinetry.
  • Materials and finishes: compare cabinet boards, shutter finishes, edge details and hardware against maintenance needs and budget.
  • Work surfaces: coordinate countertop, sink, backsplash and lighting selections with installation requirements.

If you are also planning wardrobes, TV units or a crockery unit, review the finishes together so the kitchen connects with the living and dining spaces.

What affects modular kitchen cost in Bangalore?

Cabinet quantities, shutter finishes, drawer systems, countertop selection and changes to plumbing or wiring all affect the quote. Two kitchens with a similar floor area can have different costs because their storage and material specifications differ.

Ask for an itemized scope that separates cabinetry, accessories, civil work, appliances and installation. Check which items you will supply and which Siddaspace will coordinate. Use the home interior quote planner for initial planning, then request a measured kitchen quotation.

From measurements to installation

  1. Share the floor plan, cooking needs, appliance list and preferred budget.
  2. Confirm site measurements, layout and service points.
  3. Approve drawings, materials, hardware and the written scope.
  4. Coordinate production, site readiness and installation dates.
  5. Review alignment, drawer operation and finish details at handover.

Timelines depend on design approvals, material availability and site work. Confirm the schedule for your kitchen before work begins.

Frequently asked questions

Can the kitchen match my wardrobes and living-room furniture?

Yes. Review kitchen, wardrobe and furniture finishes together when they are part of a coordinated home brief.

What affects modular kitchen cost in Bangalore?

Cabinet dimensions, shutter finish, hardware, accessories, countertop and service changes determine the scope. Ask for itemised quantities and clear inclusions.

Are appliances included in a kitchen quote?

Only where the written quotation includes them. Confirm responsibility for the hob, chimney, sink, appliances and countertop before approving production.

Can you replace a kitchen in an existing apartment?

Yes. Existing cabinet removal, wall condition, plumbing and electrical points need assessment before the replacement layout and schedule are fixed.
